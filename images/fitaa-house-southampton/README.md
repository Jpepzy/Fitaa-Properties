# Fitaa House Southampton – photos

Flat 1, Ibex House, 7 The Carronades, Southampton.

Layout facts for anyone writing copy from these photos:
- **One bedroom.** The bed is zip-and-link: it splits into two singles or joins into one large double. `bedroom-as-double*` and `bedroom-as-twin` are the same room in its two set-ups.
- **Living room** with a velvet sofa bed (`living-room-sofa-bed.jpg` shows it opened).
- **Full kitchen**: oven, hob, extractor, microwave, air fryer, fridge-freezer, washing machine.
- Breakfast bar for two by the front windows; welcome snack and hot-drinks basket.
- Utility cupboard with iron, ironing board, airer and hangers.
- No smoking.

`exterior-ibex-house-entrance.jpg` is the building entrance and is the best "arrival" shot.
